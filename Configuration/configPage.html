Peanut Butter & Jelly

Tags music from the Deezer Music API, pulls LRCLIB lyrics and artist bios/photos, and rematches playlists. Runs as a scheduled task — Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks.

Off = fill missing only. On = replace genres, artists, lyrics, photos, and bios from sources.
Never writes the Explicit tag on albums (Jellyfin would copy it onto every track).
