Peanut Butter & Jelly

Deezer → Jellyfin. Genres, artists, dates, track numbers, labels, covers, and Deezer IDs stay in sync from the API. Explicit tagging is optional (Deezer provides it). Lyrics and bios are separate sources you can turn off — Deezer does not have those.

Off = fill missing lyrics, bios, album covers, and artist photos only. On = replace those even when Jellyfin already has them. Deezer genres, artists, dates, track numbers, labels, and IDs always update when they differ.
Writes genres, album artists, song artists, release date, label, UPC, Deezer ID, and album cover (cover fills in when missing). Album artists and song artists go to separate Jellyfin fields. The album title is never given an explicit mark (that orphans tracks in Jellyfin). Explicit tags are never written on albums either; leftover ones are stripped.
Writes song artists from Deezer track credits, album artists from the album, genres, release date, track/disc numbers, ISRC, and Deezer ID. Unmatched songs get DeezerNoMatch; that tag drops when a match shows up.
Snapshots membership, then rematches by library-relative path. Empty playlists can be rebuilt from Jellyfin cleanup logs after a library move.

Explicit

Jellyfin tags go on tracks only — albums never get them (Jellyfin would copy them onto every track) and leftover album tags are stripped. Title marks can still be applied to albums and tracks. Unknown Deezer values are left alone.
